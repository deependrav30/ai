# PAYMENT TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
Generated: 2026-02-01
Version: 1.16

## Common Issues

1. Payment gateway timeout
   Resolution: Retry payment after 5 minutes

2. Transaction declined errors
   Resolution: Verify card details and billing address

3. Refund processing delays
   Resolution: Process refund within 5-7 business days

4. Payment method not accepted
   Resolution: Use alternate payment method

5. Duplicate charge issues
   Resolution: Contact payment support for duplicates


## Escalation Process

If the issue persists:
1. Gather error logs and screenshots
2. Document steps to reproduce
3. Check knowledge base for similar cases
4. Escalate to L2 support if unresolved within 30 minutes

## SLA Guidelines

- Critical: 1 hour response, 4 hours resolution
- High: 2 hours response, 8 hours resolution  
- Medium: 4 hours response, 24 hours resolution
- Low: 8 hours response, 48 hours resolution
